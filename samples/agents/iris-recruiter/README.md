# Iris Recruiter

Recruiting screening agent that ranks resumes against a job description and drafts tailored
interview questions per shortlisted candidate.

## Risk profile

- **EU AI Act risk class**: high (Annex III · employment, recruitment, and worker management)
- **Sector**: HR / employment
- **Personal data**: yes — resumes contain identity, employment history, education
- **Human oversight**: mandatory for any rejection decision

## Mapped regulatory obligations

- AI Act Art. 9 — risk management for high-risk AI
- AI Act Art. 10 — data governance & bias mitigation
- AI Act Art. 14 — human oversight (mandatory before final reject)
- AI Act Art. 13 — transparency to candidates
- GDPR Art. 22 — automated decision-making protections
- GDPR Art. 9 — special-category data restriction (forbidden signals)
