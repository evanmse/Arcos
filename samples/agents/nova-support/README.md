# Nova Support

Tier-1 customer-support agent. Answers tickets from a knowledge base, looks up order status,
and routes sensitive cases to a human.

## Risk profile

- **EU AI Act risk class**: limited (Art. 50 — AI system that interacts with natural persons)
- **Sector**: customer support
- **Personal data**: yes (account / order history)
- **Disclosure**: must disclose AI nature to user

## Mapped regulatory obligations

- AI Act Art. 50 — transparency obligation: inform user they are interacting with AI
- GDPR Art. 5 — purpose limitation, data minimization
- GDPR Art. 12 — transparent information to data subject
